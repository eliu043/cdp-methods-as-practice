Speculations on Shared Infrastructural Life

A research structure for moving between planetary systems, material evidence, institutional protocols, and possible forms of collective negotiation.

Premise

Infrastructure is not a background.

It is an active arrangement of matter, measurement, labor, law, and expectation.

The project begins where landscapes are made legible as capacity: where water becomes supply, land becomes inventory, energy becomes load, and collective futures become forecasts.

Rather than fixing the work to one municipality, the research follows the protocols through which planetary processes become sites and executable decisions.

Research question

What happens between prediction and construction?

  1. How do institutions translate planetary pressures into local sites and executable decisions?
  2. What architectures materialize futures that have been forecast but have not yet arrived?
  3. How might an atlas reveal the values embedded in models of risk, capacity, and suitability?
  4. Can an interface produce friction, obligation, or refusal instead of optimization?

Subsidiary questions

Who defines suitability? What does a model permit us to ignore? When should calculation stop?

Mechanism / anticipation

Forecasts begin building before buildings do.

Population projections, electrical-load requests, suitability scores, zoning maps, tax incentives, and infrastructure plans do not merely predict growth. They help produce it.

Once anticipated demand enters a plan, utility forecast, development agreement, or land transaction, it can begin reorganizing a landscape before construction occurs.

How does a forecast acquire material force?

“We clamor for the right to opacity for everyone.”

Making a site

A landscape becomes actionable through four translations.

Property

Boundary → title → value

Survey and law translate continuous ground into an ownable, comparable, and financeable object.

Suitability

Terrain → score → selection

Layered evidence is weighted and reduced until a landscape appears to recommend a particular use.

Capacity

Resource → service → commitment

Water, electricity, roads, and fiber become reservable capacities that prepare a site for future demand.

Demand

Projection → obligation → build

An uncertain forecast enters plans, agreements, and budgets, acquiring material force before it is verified.

Site / development front

North Texas makes these translations visible.

The project is situated among utility territories, watersheds, agricultural systems, logistics landscapes, extraction zones, industrial campuses, data centers, cable routes, and atmospheric effects.

North Texas and Red Oak remain possible apertures rather than fixed conclusions: places where residential growth, economic-development policy, agricultural land, and hyperscale computation visibly meet.

Methods and materials

Read each layer beside what it omits.

Spatial and quantitative

Land-cover change, parcels, permits, zoning, utility territories, grid forecasts, water records, transport and cable routes, aerial and satellite imagery.

Institutional and qualitative

Public testimony, meeting minutes, development agreements, standards, maintenance manuals, environmental records, field observation, and interviews.

Computational and performative

Competing suitability models, layered atlases, interface friction, protocols of obligation, visible uncertainty, and moments of computational refusal.

Visual representation

The interface must show what the model suppresses.

The visual language draws from atlases, planning documents, technical manuals, evidentiary practices, artists’ books, and open research canvases.

Images retain their status as evidence rather than decoration. Captions, provenance, scale, and uncertainty remain visible. Scrolling, panning, dragging, and withholding allow the reader to encounter an argument rather than merely receive it.

“Blacklight does not illuminate: it makes things emanate or shine.”

Denise Ferreira da Silva, Blacklight

Provisional claim

Uncertainty becomes commitment.

Growth becomes actionable only after institutions translate it into forecasts, zoning categories, infrastructure plans, financial incentives, and suitable sites.

The subdivision and the data-center campus are two architectures of anticipated demand. One materializes forecasts of future residents; the other materializes forecasts of future computation.

These futures are not inevitable. They are governed through protocols that can be examined, contested, and potentially rewritten.

Potential capstone

An atlas that keeps the decision open.

Regional

Trace land conversion, residential construction, data-center development, and infrastructure expansion.

Municipal

Examine where agricultural land, residential growth, economic policy, and hyperscale computation meet.

Parcel

Reconstruct how selected landscapes became sites for houses, servers, substations, roads, and investment.

A covenant-based interface acts as friction. It asks the user to confront testimony, ecological duration, water obligations, maintenance, or uncertainty before proceeding.

In some situations, it may prevent calculation altogether.

Negotiation

Can a map become a site of negotiation before a forecast becomes a commitment?
